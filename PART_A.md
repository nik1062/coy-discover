# Part A: Two feature suggestions for Coy

## 1. Crush status tracker with an anonymous re-nudge
**What I noticed:** After adding a crush, the card shows only name, phone and email. I can't tell whether the SMS/email arrived or whether they joined. The home card says "No one has added you yet", which gives no reason to come back.

**Feature:** Show a status per crush (sent, delivered, joined, mutual) and offer an optional anonymous reminder after 3-5 days. Identity stays hidden, as today.

**Why:** It removes the biggest dead end in the product, gives users a reason to return, and turns crushes into new signups, which feeds the referral loop.

## 2. Group-ready invite cards with attribution and partial progress
**What I noticed:** The share sheet sends plain text ("Someone in this group might have a crush on you 👀…"). The free slot needs 3 friends to join *and* add a crush, with no progress shown per friend.

**Feature:** A link-preview card for WhatsApp/Instagram groups, per-link tracking, and partial progress (for example a half slot when one friend joins).

**Why:** Coy spreads in group chats, so better previews raise click-through. A visibly achievable free path increases invites before users reach the ₹129 paywall.

*Also considered:* block/report controls, since anyone can add anyone's number.
